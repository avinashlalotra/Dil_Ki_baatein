# The Three Crimes

```
One day, an unusual complaint arrived in the king's royal court.
A man came forward as the complainant, 
claiming that a certain young woman had been troubling him greatly —
and that she was also a thief.
Both the complainant and the young woman were summoned before the court. 
The woman flatly denied every single accusation.
```

`Complainant`: "Your Majesty, this woman has been torturing me for a while."

`King`: "Lady, why are you bothering this poor man ?"

`Woman`: "I beg your pardon, Your Majesty. I haven't done anything, and I have hardly known him for the past five months."

`Complainant`: "Your Majesty, I thought she was a thief only, but now I can see a liar too ."

`King`: " What has she done to you ?"

`Woman`: "Forgive me, Your Majesty. But I have done nothing, and this man.. He is completely out of his mind (Pagal)."

`King`: "Complainant! Speak clearly — what exactly is this woman's crime?"
